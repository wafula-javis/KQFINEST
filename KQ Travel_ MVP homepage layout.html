Before you book

Quick answers to common questions.

When is the best time to visit the Maasai Mara?

July to October for the wildebeest crossings, though wildlife is good all year.

What is the resident rate?

Kenyan citizens and residents pay lower park and lodge rates. Use the toggle in the header and bring your ID.

How do I pay?

M-Pesa, card, or bank transfer. A deposit confirms your booking.

Can I change my dates?

Yes, free up to 14 days before departure. Terms will be confirmed in your quote.
